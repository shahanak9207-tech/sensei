# Run HidePix in VS Code (Windows)

HidePix is a funny prank demo with a photo trap, meme refusal screens, puzzles, fake close friends notifications, and cute voice-overs. Everything runs 100% locally and safely on your computer.

## 1. How to Run

Open your terminal in VS Code (`Ctrl + \``) or PowerShell in this folder and run:

```powershell
py -m pip install -r requirements.txt
py hidepix.py
```
*(If `py` is not found, use `python hidepix.py`)*

## 2. Full Demo Guide (For Presentations & TinkerHub 🎤)

1. **FrontPage (Cartoon Decorative UI)**:
   - Notice the colorful bubblegum cartoon styling and the **`⚠️ DEMO MODE: USELESS PRANK EDITION ⚠️`** badge.
   - Enter your name under **"👤 Your Name for Gossip"**.
   - Click **"🎲 Use Sample Prank Photo"** or **"📁 Select My Photo"** to see it previewed in the central comic vault frame.

2. **The Photo Trap (`HidePhotoTrap`)**:
   - Click **"🔒 Hide My Photo (Trap!) 🚀"**.
   - Your desktop wallpaper is immediately set to the photo!
   - A fake phone toast appears: *"Close Friends Alert: [Name] just hid a photo by broadcasting it across their desktop!"*
   - A cute voice plays: *"Nope! I'm not deleting that!"*
   - The **5-Minute Reset Lock** starts ticking! ⏳

3. **Close Friends Mystery Section (`CloseFriendsSection`)**:
   - Click **"👥 Close Friends Section (Mystery) 🤫"**.
   - Add/remove emails from the local list.
   - Read **The Grand Mystery**: No emails are ever sent; they are saved only to make you panic during the demo!

4. **The Meme Screens (`MemeScreens`)**:
   - Try clicking **"🔒 Reset Wallpaper"** while the timer is still ticking.
   - The app refuses with a randomized cartoon meme screen (e.g. *“Snake says: Sssstop rushing! 🐍”*, *“Error 418: I'm a teapot 🫖”*, *“Separation Anxiety 🥺”*).

5. **The Puzzle Trap (`PuzzleTrap`)**:
   - Click **"🧠 Solve Puzzle to Unlock Early 🧩"**.
   - Solve the riddle and the Snake math trick question (`2 + 2 × 0 + 1 = 3`).
   - Click **Submit**: The app plays voice *"Wow nice try!"* and pops up: *“It was fun... but STILL A PRANK! 🐍 Wallpaper is NOT being reset early!”*

6. **Reset Timer & Snake Unlock (`ResetTimer`)**:
   - Wait 5 minutes OR click the **`⚡ Demo Skip (0s)`** button (built specifically so presenters don't have to wait 5 real minutes in front of judges).
   - The Cartoon Snake popup appears: *"Sssssurvived! 🐍 Since you waited 5 minutes, forgiveness is granted!"*
   - Click **"🎉 Snake Unlocked Reset!"** to restore your original desktop wallpaper.

7. **Cute Voice-Over Replay (`CuteVoiceOver`)**:
   - Click **"🔊 Replay Voice: 'Nope!' 🎤"** anytime to play the cute audio line.

## 3. Safety & Privacy Guarantee

> **100% Offline & Safe**: HidePix never connects to the internet, uploads zero files, and never sends emails. All contacts are kept locally in `friends_list.json`.
